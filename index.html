Where's Fly-do?

Ten fruit flies learning to find the striped guy. Each one sees through an eye with a real fly's facet count and learns with mushroom bodies wired from that fly's real connectome. Land on him: sugar. Land on anyone else: a zap.

Watching Fly A

The lab

Ten flies with the same real brain wiring, copied from one fly, so any difference between them comes from experience, like identical twins raised apart. The fly that has found him the most leads. Pick one to watch it.

What is it like to be a fly?

Flies barely see red, so his red-and-white stripes look black-and-white, just like the mime's. To a fly, the mime is his shadow.
