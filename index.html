Where's Fly-do?

Ten fruit flies learning to find the striped guy. Each one sees through an eye with a real fly's facet count and learns with mushroom bodies sized from a real fly's wiring diagram. Land on him: sugar. Land on anyone else: a zap.

The lab

Ten flies, each born with its own random wiring. Same world, same rules, different brains. The fly that has found him the most leads.

What is it like to be a fly?

Flies barely see red, so his red-and-white stripes look black-and-white, just like the mime's. To a fly, the mime is his shadow.
